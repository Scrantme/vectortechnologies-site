Your engineers became the filter

Generative AI lets candidates mass-produce tailored CVs and solve take-home coding tests in minutes. The old screens no longer separate engineers who can build from those who can prompt. So the screening moved downstream, onto your most expensive and most constrained people. Tech leads spend their week live-debugging basic loops on video calls instead of designing systems and shipping product.

How it works

  1. Book.

    Your Talent team picks the candidate's role and seniority, and books a time. No engineer needed.

  2. Evaluate.

    A Vector evaluator runs a live, in-depth technical evaluation over video, testing real-time problem solving and architectural judgment.

  3. Decide.

    You receive a written assessment, so only verified candidates reach your engineering team.
